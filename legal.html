Copyright concerns? Here is the process.

This page explains AniClips’ general legal position and how to submit a copyright complaint or DMCA-style takedown request. It is intended to make rights-holder requests easier to process; it is not legal advice and does not create rights or obligations beyond those imposed by applicable law.

1. Purpose of This Page

This page explains AniClips’ approach to copyright, intellectual property, and requests to remove material that may infringe someone’s rights.

AniClips is an anime clip discovery and access platform intended to help users find short clips for creative editing and related community activities. We recognize that anime footage, music, artwork, characters, and other materials may be protected by copyright or other intellectual property rights.

We take legitimate copyright concerns seriously and provide a process for rights holders and their authorized representatives to request a review. This page is informational and does not constitute legal advice.

2. Copyright Ownership

AniClips does not claim ownership of third-party anime footage or other copyrighted works merely because they are listed, processed, previewed, or made accessible through the website.

Copyright and other intellectual property rights remain with their respective owners or licensors. Accessing a clip through AniClips does not transfer ownership or grant permission from the original rights holder for every possible use.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of material complies with applicable laws and any relevant permissions or licensing requirements.

3. Intended Uses and Restrictions

AniClips is designed to support creative and community uses such as:

  • Anime edits and AMVs.
  • Fan-made videos and other transformative creative projects.
  • Commentary, criticism, reviews, and analysis.
  • Educational and other uses permitted by applicable law.

These examples are not a guarantee that every use is lawful. Copyright exceptions and limitations vary by jurisdiction, and the legality of a particular use depends on its circumstances.

The library must not be used as a deliberate means of copyright infringement, as a substitute for authorized commercial distribution, or to systematically mirror and redistribute the collection as a competing library.

Users must not misrepresent third-party works as their own or imply that AniClips grants rights that it does not possess. Please also read the AniClips Terms of Use for the rules governing access to and use of the website.

4. How to Submit a Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner, an authorized representative, or another party with a legitimate basis to report suspected infringement, you may contact us at:

Email: aniclips.site@gmail.com

Suggested subject: Copyright Takedown Request — AniClips

Include: The exact AniClips URL(s), the work you own or represent, and your contact details.

Please identify the specific material you want us to review. Providing exact URLs and sufficient information helps us locate the content and assess your request. You do not need to use a lawyer to contact us, although you may wish to obtain legal advice.

If the same work appears at multiple URLs, list each URL separately where possible. Screenshots may help explain the issue, but please include the actual URL(s) as well.

5. Information to Include in a Request

To help us evaluate the request, include as many of the following details as apply:

  • Your identity and contact details: Full name, organization (if applicable), and a working email address. A phone number or mailing address may be included if appropriate.
  • The copyrighted work: Identify the work you own or are authorized to represent. If you represent a company or rights holder, explain your authority to act for them.
  • The material in question: Provide each exact AniClips page or clip URL and enough detail to identify the specific material. General statements such as “remove all anime” are not sufficiently specific for a targeted review.
  • Why you believe it infringes: Briefly explain the rights involved and why the identified material is unauthorized. If relevant, identify the episode, title, release, or other source work.
  • Good-faith statement: State that you believe in good faith that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • Accuracy and authority statement: Confirm that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on the rights holder’s behalf, where applicable.
  • Signature: Include your typed full name as an electronic signature. A formal notice under a particular law may require additional wording or information.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, or unrelated sensitive personal information. We may ask for reasonable additional information if we cannot verify or locate the material.

6. Review and Possible Removal

After receiving a request, AniClips may assess whether it identifies the material clearly, whether the sender appears to have a legitimate basis to make the request, and whether additional information is needed.

Depending on the circumstances, we may:

  • Request clarification or additional information.
  • Temporarily restrict access while a credible complaint is reviewed, where appropriate.
  • Remove or disable access to identified material when warranted.
  • Inform the person who submitted the request of the outcome when reasonably practicable.

We aim to handle legitimate requests fairly and within a reasonable period. Processing times may vary depending on the completeness of the request, the complexity of the issue, and the availability of relevant information.

Submitting a complaint does not guarantee a particular outcome. We may be unable to act on vague, unverifiable, or incorrectly directed requests.

7. Disputes and Counter-Notices

If material is removed or restricted following a complaint and you believe this happened in error, you may contact aniclips.site@gmail.com with the relevant URL, an explanation of your position, and any supporting information.

We may review the dispute and consider restoring access where appropriate.

This informal review process is not necessarily equivalent to a formal counter-notice procedure under the laws of any particular jurisdiction. If a statutory procedure applies, its specific requirements and deadlines must be followed.

8. Repeat Infringement

We may consider repeated, credible copyright complaints when deciding what action is appropriate for a particular item, account, submission, or use of the service. Any action will depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

This policy does not promise a particular enforcement outcome or imply that AniClips has adopted a formal repeat-infringer program under a specific statute.

9. False or Abusive Complaints

Copyright complaints should be accurate, made in good faith, and limited to material the sender has a legitimate basis to report.

Knowingly submitting false information, claiming rights you do not hold, or using the takedown process to harass others may have legal consequences under applicable law.

AniClips may decline to act on requests that appear fraudulent, abusive, or unsupported, and may seek clarification before taking action.

10. Third-Party Hosting and External Services

Some material accessible through AniClips may be stored, delivered, or processed using third-party hosting providers and infrastructure.

AniClips may not directly control every external server or service involved in delivering material. Where a complaint concerns content hosted by a third party, we may review the relevant AniClips listing and, where appropriate, direct the complainant to the responsible provider or submit a separate request through that provider’s process.

A third-party hosting arrangement does not, by itself, determine whether a particular use is lawful or eliminate any obligations that may apply to AniClips.

11. No Guarantee of Availability or Rights Clearance

AniClips does not guarantee that every item in the library is available at all times, free of third-party claims, licensed for every intended use, or suitable for redistribution.

Material may be changed, restricted, or removed as the service evolves or when a legitimate legal or operational reason arises. Users who need rights-cleared material for commercial productions or other uses requiring permission should obtain the necessary authorization from the relevant rights holders.

12. Applicable Law

AniClips operates from India. Copyright complaints and disputes may involve the laws of India or other jurisdictions, depending on the parties, material, and circumstances.

References to “DMCA” describe a commonly used copyright-notice framework associated with United States law. This page does not claim that AniClips has registered a DMCA agent, qualifies for any particular statutory safe harbor, or is exempt from liability. Those matters depend on facts and legal requirements that must be assessed separately.

Nothing on this page limits any rights or remedies available under applicable law.
