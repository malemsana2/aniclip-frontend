AniClips is a clip-discovery and editing utility. It is not intended to replace official releases, streaming services, or the original works from which clips may originate.

Editing versus redistribution

The point of these restrictions is to distinguish using a clip as material for a creative work from exploiting the library as a source of media for resale or rehosting.

Adding minimal changes does not automatically make redistribution permissible. Likewise, monetizing an original edit does not automatically make it lawful or unlawful. Copyright exceptions and permissions depend on the circumstances and applicable law.
